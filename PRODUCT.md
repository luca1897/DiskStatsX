# Product

## Register

product

## Users

macOS users, developers, and power users who need to understand where local disk space is allocated. They may scan very large APFS trees and need the interface to remain responsive while they compare folders, locate large files, and decide what is safe to review or remove.

## Product Purpose

DiskStatsX provides fast, local-first disk analysis through a native macOS scanner and focused visual exploration. Success means users can scan large volumes accurately, understand storage semantics, navigate results without rescanning, and reclaim space without exposing filesystem data to external services.

## Brand Personality

Precise, focused, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a capable macOS utility: dense where comparison benefits from density, calm during long-running work, and explicit about destructive actions or storage estimates.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic cross-platform cleaner interfaces, marketing dashboards, decorative card-heavy layouts, toy-like visualizations, unexplained automation, and UI that hides filesystem scope or storage semantics.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current filesystem scope and scan state visible.
- Distinguish indexed exploration from a fresh disk scan.
- Remain responsive on datasets with millions of entries.
- Explain allocated, logical, shared, cloud-only, and historical storage honestly.
- Make destructive actions deliberate, specific, and recoverable where possible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Preserve visible focus, avoid color-only state communication, support reduced motion, and keep error and confirmation messages readable with assistive technology.
